## What we built
- Primary workflow: `user/default/workflows/wan22_i2v_pro_chain3_endframe_720p.json`
- 3-link I2V chain, each link 81 frames @ 16 fps.
- **Mid-chain waypoint**: link 2 = `WanFirstLastFrameToVideo` with `end_image` from a LoadImage (node 44). Lets you steer toward a known target frame WITHOUT knowing the true video end. Link 3 free-runs from link 2's last frame.
- Sampler per link: HIGH 0→2 (noise enable, leftover enable) then LOW 2→10000 (noise disable, leftover disable), euler/simple, CFG 1.0. **4 total steps** optimal with lightx2v distill LoRAs.
